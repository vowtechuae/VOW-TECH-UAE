// The Data Risk Reality

Data Risks Every UAE Business Faces

Data is your most valuable asset — and one of your most exposed. Whether through accidental deletion, ransomware, or insider misuse, unprotected data puts your entire business at risk.

Data Loss & Corruption

Hardware failure, accidental deletion, ransomware, and software bugs can permanently destroy business-critical data in seconds. Without robust backup and recovery, the impact can be catastrophic and irreversible.

Unauthorised Access & Data Breaches

Weak access controls, unencrypted storage, and over-privileged accounts leave sensitive data exposed to both external attackers and malicious insiders — putting customer trust and regulatory compliance at serious risk.

Human Error & Compliance Gaps

Employees mishandling, misdirecting, or accidentally exposing sensitive data is the leading cause of data incidents in the UAE. Without DLP policies and audit trails, you cannot detect, prevent, or prove what happened.

Our Data Protection Solutions

A complete portfolio of data protection technologies — securing your data at rest, in transit, and in use across every environment.

Data Encryption

AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS/SSL for data in transit — ensuring sensitive files, databases, and communications remain unreadable to anyone without authorisation, even if storage media is compromised.

AES-256 • BitLocker • TLS 1.3

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Automated, policy-driven backup schedules with off-site and cloud replication — coupled with tested DR runbooks that guarantee your data can be recovered within your defined RTO and RPO targets.

Veeam • Acronis • Immutable Backup

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Policy-based controls that detect and prevent sensitive data from leaving your environment — blocking unauthorised email attachments, USB transfers, cloud uploads, and print actions in real time.

Email DLP • Endpoint DLP • Cloud DLP

Identity & Access Management (IAM)

Role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, and single sign-on — ensuring every user has exactly the permissions they need, nothing more, with every access event fully logged and auditable.

MFA • RBAC • Azure AD • SSO

Data Classification & Governance

Automated discovery and classification of sensitive data across your environment — labelling files by sensitivity, applying appropriate handling policies, and generating the audit evidence regulators require.

Microsoft Purview • Sensitivity Labels

Continuous Monitoring & Alerting

Real-time monitoring of data access patterns, backup job status, policy violations, and anomalous behaviour — with automated alerting so your team can respond to incidents before they escalate.

SIEM Integration • Audit Logs • Alerts

Endpoint Data Protection

Device encryption, remote wipe capability, and endpoint DLP agents ensuring data on laptops, mobile devices, and BYOD equipment remains protected — even when devices are lost, stolen, or compromised.

Intune • MDM • Remote Wipe

Cloud Data Security

Security controls for Microsoft 365, Azure, AWS, and private cloud — including Cloud Access Security Broker (CASB) deployment, shadow IT discovery, and cloud DLP policy enforcement.

// How We Work

Our Data Protection Methodology

A proven five-phase approach that identifies data risks, implements controls, and keeps your information protected long-term.

01

Data Assessment

A comprehensive audit of where your sensitive data lives, how it flows, who can access it, and what protections are already in place — establishing your baseline risk posture.

02

Classification & Policy

We classify your data by sensitivity and business value, then design the access, handling, retention, and encryption policies that govern how each category is protected and used.

03

Controls Implementation

Deployment of encryption, IAM, DLP, backup, and monitoring solutions — configured to your policy framework and integrated with your existing IT environment with minimal operational disruption.

04

Testing & Validation

Backup recovery drills, DLP policy testing, access control audits, and encryption verification — confirming every control works as intended before you rely on it in a real incident.

05

Monitoring & Compliance

Ongoing monitoring of data access, backup health, policy violations, and regulatory posture — with regular reporting and annual reviews to keep your protections current as your business evolves.

Data protection specifically addresses the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your data itself — through encryption, backup, access control, and DLP. The two disciplines are complementary and both are essential. VOWTECH provides both as part of a complete security posture, and we'll help you understand where each applies in your environment.
