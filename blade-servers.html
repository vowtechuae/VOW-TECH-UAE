// THE RISKS

Why Traditional Rack Servers Limit Growth

Conventional 1U/2U rack servers bring sprawl, wasted energy, and operational complexity that blade architecture is purpose-built to solve.

01
Power & Cooling Sprawl

Individual rack servers each carry their own power supplies, fans, and cables — multiplying energy draw, heat output, and failure points. Data centres routinely waste 30–40% of power on inefficient per-server infrastructure. Blade chassis share resources, cutting this drastically.

02
Cable & Management Complexity

A rack of 20 servers generates hundreds of power, network, and management cables — a nightmare to trace, maintain, and audit. A single blade chassis replaces all of that with a clean internal fabric, dramatically simplifying administration and reducing mean-time-to-repair.

03
Slow, Disruptive Scaling

Expanding traditional server infrastructure means racking new units, re-cabling, reconfiguring switches, and potential downtime windows. Blade architecture allows you to slide a new blade into a vacant slot — online, in minutes — without touching a single cable outside the chassis.
