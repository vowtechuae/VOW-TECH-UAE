Common Malware & Spam Threats

Unprotected endpoints and inboxes are the most common entry point for the threats that disrupt UAE businesses every day.

Ransomware & File-Encrypting Malware

A single infected endpoint without active anti-virus protection can encrypt shared drives and cripple operations within minutes — often demanding payment for recovery.

Spam-Borne Malware Delivery

The majority of malware still arrives via spam email attachments and links — making gateway-level spam filtering your first and most effective line of defence.

Fileless & Zero-Day Malware

Modern malware increasingly evades signature-only antivirus by living in memory or exploiting unpatched vulnerabilities — requiring behavioural and heuristic detection to catch.
