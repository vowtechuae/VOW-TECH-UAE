// THE THREAT LANDSCAPE

What UAE Businesses Face Every Day

The UAE is one of the most targeted regions for cyber attacks globally — with sophisticated threat actors targeting businesses of every size across every sector.

01
Ransomware & Malware

Ransomware attacks in the UAE increased by over 78% in 2024, with attackers targeting SMEs and mid-market organisations that lack enterprise security controls. A successful ransomware attack encrypts business-critical data, halts operations, and demands payment — often with no guarantee of recovery even if paid. VOWTECH's layered security approach — including endpoint detection, network segmentation, and immutable backups — significantly reduces ransomware risk and ensures recovery capability if an attack succeeds.

02
Phishing & Business Email Compromise

Phishing remains the single most common initial attack vector — with business email compromise (BEC) attacks targeting UAE organisations for fraudulent wire transfers, credential theft, and supply chain infiltration. Sophisticated spear-phishing campaigns impersonate executives, vendors, and banks with increasing accuracy. VOWTECH's email security, anti-phishing training, and multi-factor authentication deployments significantly reduce the risk of successful phishing attacks reaching and deceiving your users.

03
Insider Threats & Data Theft

Insider threats — whether malicious employees, compromised accounts, or negligent data handling — represent a significant and often underestimated risk. Data Loss Prevention tools, privileged access management, activity monitoring, and least-privilege access controls are essential to detecting and preventing insider data theft. VOWTECH designs and implements DLP and PAM solutions that protect sensitive data without impeding legitimate business operations.

04
Unpatched Systems & Vulnerabilities

Attackers actively scan for and exploit known vulnerabilities in unpatched systems — operating systems, applications, firmware, and network devices. Many UAE organisations run systems that are months or years behind on security patches, providing attackers with well-documented exploitation paths. VOWTECH's managed patching and vulnerability management services ensure your systems are kept current and exposure windows are minimised before exploitation occurs.

05
Unsecured Wireless Networks

Improperly configured wireless networks — using outdated encryption, no network segmentation, or weak authentication — provide attackers with direct access to internal systems from outside your physical perimeter. Rogue access points, evil twin attacks, and guest network bridges are common attack vectors in office environments. VOWTECH designs and secures enterprise wireless environments with proper segmentation, WPA3, and wireless intrusion detection that monitors for unauthorised activity in real time.

06
Regulatory Non-Compliance

UAE businesses face a growing body of data protection and cyber security regulations — including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), NESA controls, ADIO requirements, and sector-specific frameworks in financial services and healthcare. Non-compliance carries legal exposure, contractual penalties, and reputational damage. VOWTECH's security implementations are aligned with UAE regulatory requirements, providing the documented controls and audit trail evidence that compliance assessments require.
