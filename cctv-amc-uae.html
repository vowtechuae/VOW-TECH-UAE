// OVERVIEW

One Contract, Every Site Held to the Same Standard

CCTV tends to be bought site by site. The Abu Dhabi head office has one installer, the Dubai showroom another, and the warehouse in a third emirate was fitted out by whoever built it. Years later nobody at head office can say with confidence which cameras work, how many days of footage each site keeps or who to call when a recorder fails. A multi-site CCTV AMC replaces that patchwork with one agreement and one standard.

VOWTECH is based in Abu Dhabi, and that is where our engineers travel from. For a Dubai office or warehouse, CCTV AMC works just as it does under our CCTV AMC in Abu Dhabi: preventive visits are scheduled in advance, and between visits we carry out remote health checks wherever the recorder can be reached securely. CCTV maintenance in Dubai and the other emirates is planned by route so several sites are covered in one trip.

Because recorders, switches and remote access are IT components, multi-site CCTV care sits naturally beside wider IT cover. Companies that already use our IT support for Dubai offices or managed IT services across the UAE can add surveillance to the same relationship, and new or replacement systems are handled by our CCTV installation team.

// FAQ

Multi-Site CCTV AMC Questions

Can one CCTV AMC cover sites in different emirates?

Yes. A single contract can list any number of locations, each with its own equipment schedule and visit frequency. The terms, checklist and reporting format are common to all sites, which is what gives head office a consistent view. Sites can be added to or removed from the contract as the business changes.

Do you provide CCTV AMC and CCTV maintenance in Dubai?

Yes. VOWTECH is based in Abu Dhabi and does not have a Dubai office; our engineers travel to Dubai for scheduled preventive visits and fault attendance, and we use remote health checks between visits where the recorder can be accessed securely. One-off CCTV maintenance in Dubai can also be arranged.

How do remote CCTV health checks work?

With your approval we set up a secure method of reaching each recorder, typically through a VPN or the manufacturer's cloud service rather than open ports. We then log in periodically to check camera status, disk health, recording and time settings. Anything that needs physical attention is scheduled for a visit.

How often are sites visited under a multi-site contract?

The frequency is set per site in the contract. Quarterly visits are common for indoor office systems, and monthly visits are often chosen for outdoor, dusty or high-risk locations. A mixed schedule is normal — a busy warehouse may be visited more often than a small branch office.

What if our sites use different camera and recorder brands?

That is the usual situation and it does not prevent a single contract. The checklist is brand-neutral, and the asset register records what is at each site. Over time we may suggest standardising on fewer platforms as equipment is replaced, because it simplifies spares, training and remote access.

What reports will head office receive?

After each visit you receive a service report for that site listing what was checked, faults found, work done and recommendations. These can be summarised across all locations so management can see open issues and ageing equipment at a glance. Our guide what is CCTV AMC covers reporting in more detail.
