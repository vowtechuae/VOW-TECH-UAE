// POLICY RISKS

Risks of Lacking Proper Network Policies

Without clearly defined, consistently enforced network security policies, organisations lose visibility and control over how their IT infrastructure is accessed, configured, and protected.

01
Unauthorized Access & Breaches

Without formal access policies, accounts, devices, and third parties can reach systems they were never meant to touch — leaving the door open for both external attackers and unintentional internal exposure.

02
Policy Violations & Misconfigurations

Undocumented or inconsistently applied rules lead to configuration drift across firewalls, switches, and servers — small misconfigurations that quietly accumulate into serious exploitable weaknesses.

03
Service Disruptions & Downtime

Ad-hoc network changes made without a governing policy framework often introduce conflicts and outages — disrupting business operations at the worst possible moment.

04
Lack of Documentation

When security rules exist only in individual engineers' heads, staff turnover leaves organisations without a record of why systems are configured the way they are — making audits and troubleshooting far harder.

05
Regulatory Non-Compliance

Many UAE regulatory and industry frameworks require documented, enforced security policies. Operating without them risks audit failures, fines, and reputational damage during a compliance review.

06
Slow Threat Response

Without predefined incident response and escalation policies, teams waste critical time deciding how to react to a live threat — time that materially increases the impact of a breach.
