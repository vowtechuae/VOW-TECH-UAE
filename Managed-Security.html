// THREATS YOU FACE

Why Businesses Need Managed Security

Most internal IT teams aren't built for round-the-clock threat hunting. Without dedicated managed security, gaps in coverage become the openings attackers rely on.

01
No Coverage Outside Office Hours

Cyberattacks don't wait for business hours. Without 24/7 monitoring, threats that strike overnight or on weekends go unnoticed until staff return — by which point ransomware has often already spread across the network and encrypted critical systems.

02
Unprotected Endpoints

Laptops, desktops, and mobile devices are the most common entry point for attackers. Without managed endpoint protection, outdated antivirus, missing patches, and unmonitored devices leave the door open for malware, ransomware, and credential theft.

03
Slow Detection & Response

In-house teams often lack the tooling and bandwidth to detect threats quickly. The longer a breach goes unnoticed, the more damage it causes — every hour of delay increases the cost and complexity of containment and recovery.

04
Compliance & Audit Pressure

UAE regulations increasingly require demonstrable security monitoring and incident response capability. Businesses without managed security struggle to produce the logs, reports, and evidence regulators and auditors demand.

05
Security Skills Shortage

Qualified security analysts are scarce and expensive to hire and retain. Most SMEs and mid-size organisations cannot justify a full in-house SOC team — leaving security as a part-time responsibility bolted onto an already stretched IT department.

06
Fragmented Security Tools

Firewalls, antivirus, and email filters running in isolation, with no one correlating alerts across them, create blind spots. Threats that slip between disconnected tools are exactly the ones that cause the most damage before anyone notices.
