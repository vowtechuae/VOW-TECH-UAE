What is an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) and what does it include?
An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) from VOWTECH is a comprehensive IT support agreement that covers your entire IT infrastructure for a fixed annual fee. It typically includes: unlimited helpdesk support, scheduled preventive maintenance visits, priority response times for emergencies, hardware and software troubleshooting, network monitoring, and regular health-check reports. AMC removes the unpredictability of ad-hoc IT costs and ensures your IT environment is proactively maintained rather than reactively fixed.

What is penetration testing and does my UAE business need it?
Penetration testing (pen testing) is a controlled, authorised simulated cyberattack on your IT systems — designed to identify vulnerabilities before real attackers do. If your business handles sensitive customer data, processes payments, operates in a regulated sector, or holds government contracts in the UAE, penetration testing is increasingly required for compliance. VOWTECH conducts external network pen tests, internal network assessments, web application security testing, and social engineering assessments — providing a detailed report of findings and prioritised remediation guidance.

How does Veeam backup work and what does VOWTECH offer?
Veeam is an enterprise backup and disaster recovery platform that protects virtual machines, physical servers, and cloud workloads. We also configure immutable backup copies that ransomware cannot encrypt.

Biometrics & Access Control
What face recognition attendance systems do you install in UAE?
VOWTECH deploys AI-powered face recognition attendance systems from ZKTeco, Suprema, and Hikvision across the UAE. These systems provide contactless biometric attendance recording, liveness detection to prevent spoofing, mask-wearing compatibility, and cloud-based management. We integrate attendance data directly with HR and payroll systems (SAP, Oracle, MS Dynamics) to eliminate manual processing. Systems achieve 99.5% recognition accuracy with sub-0.5-second identification speed.
Can face recognition prevent buddy punching in my business?
Yes — buddy punching (one employee clocking in for an absent colleague) is completely eliminated by face recognition attendance systems. Because the system verifies the physical biometric identity of the individual at the terminal, it is impossible to clock in on behalf of someone else regardless of any collusion between employees. For organisations with more than 20 staff, the cost savings from eliminated buddy punching alone typically recover the full system cost within 12 months of deployment.
