// MIGRATION RISKS

Risks of an Unplanned Relocation

Moving an office without a structured IT plan puts servers, data, and daily operations at risk — and can turn a straightforward relocation into weeks of disruption.

01
Poor Planning & Delays

Without a detailed site survey and migration schedule, moves run over time and budget — leaving staff without working systems for longer than necessary.

02
Network & Service Downtime

Uncoordinated cutovers between old and new premises can leave email, internet, and line-of-business applications offline during critical business hours.

03
Data & System Risks

Servers and storage moved without proper backup and handling procedures risk data loss, corruption, or hardware damage in transit.

04
Equipment Damage in Transit

Servers, switches, and workstations packed and transported without the right protocols are exposed to physical damage that can be costly to replace.

05
Staff Productivity Loss

Employees left without working desks, phones, or logins on day one lose valuable working hours — and first impressions with clients can suffer too.

06
Vendor Coordination Gaps

ISPs, landlords, and building management all need coordinated lead times — missed handoffs between vendors are one of the most common causes of migration delays.

// HOW WE WORK

Our 5-Step Migration Process

A structured relocation workflow — from site survey through testing and handover — designed to keep your business running before, during, and after the move.

01
Site Survey & Planning

We assess your new location and build a detailed migration timeline around your business hours and priorities.

02
Backup & Preparation

Full data backups are taken and verified, and equipment is prepared and labelled ahead of disconnection.

03
Equipment Packing & Transport

Servers, workstations, and networking gear are securely packed and transported using protective handling procedures.

04
Installation & Configuration

Equipment is reinstalled, cabled, and configured at the new site, with every connection checked against the original setup.

05
Testing & Handover

We test every system end-to-end and remain on-site through go-live, then hand over with full documentation and support.
