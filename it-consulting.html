Our IT Consulting Services

Expert guidance across every dimension of IT strategy, security, compliance, and operational resilience — delivered by VOWTECH's senior consultants for UAE businesses of every size.

01
IT Audit & Health Check

A comprehensive assessment of your entire IT environment — infrastructure, security, backups, performance, compliance, and licensing — producing a prioritised risk register and 12-month improvement roadmap. VOWTECH audits cover 120+ checkpoints and are delivered with a full written report and debrief session within 48 hours of on-site assessment.

Infrastructure Security Risk Register

02
Vulnerability Assessment

Structured vulnerability scanning and manual testing across your network, applications, servers, and endpoints — identifying exploitable weaknesses before threat actors do. Each finding is risk-rated (Critical / High / Medium / Low) with a clear technical description, affected asset, potential impact, and specific remediation guidance your team can act on immediately.

Network Scan Endpoint Web Apps

03
Security Risk Assessment

A formal risk assessment mapping your threat landscape, identifying your most valuable and vulnerable assets, and quantifying the likelihood and business impact of each identified risk. The output is a structured risk treatment plan aligned to your organisation's risk appetite — giving leadership the evidence needed to make informed security investment decisions and satisfy audit or regulatory requirements.

Threat Modelling Risk Rating Treatment Plan

04
Information Security Consultancy

Strategic information security guidance covering policy development, security architecture design, access control frameworks, data classification, encryption standards, third-party risk management, and ISO 27001 alignment. Our consultants help you build an information security programme that is proportionate to your business risk and practical to implement — not an academic exercise that sits on a shelf unused.

ISO 27001 Policy Data Protection

Quality & Compliance Consulting

Expert compliance consulting helping UAE businesses meet regulatory and contractual obligations across IT, data protection, and information security domains — including UAE PDPL, CBUAE, DOH, ADGM, ISO standards, and PCI-DSS. We conduct gap analyses, prepare compliance documentation, support evidence gathering, and help your team navigate audit and certification processes with minimal disruption to operations.

UAE PDPL PCI-DSS ISO Standards

06
IT Cost Management

Structured analysis of your IT expenditure — licences, subscriptions, hardware refresh cycles, cloud spend, vendor contracts, and support costs — identifying consolidation, renegotiation, and elimination opportunities. VOWTECH IT cost reviews consistently identify between 15% and 35% avoidable annual IT spend, with a clear cost reduction roadmap that maintains or improves capability while reducing overall IT operating cost.

Licence Review Cloud Optimisation Cost Reduction

Business Continuity Strategy

Development and testing of a practical Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and IT Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) that your team can actually execute under pressure — covering critical system identification, recovery time and recovery point objectives, failover procedures, backup validation, communication protocols, and tabletop exercise facilitation to verify your plan works before you need it in a real incident.

BCP / DRP RTO / RPO DR Testing

08
IT Requirement Analysis

Structured requirements gathering and analysis for IT projects, technology procurement, and digital transformation initiatives — translating business objectives into clear technical specifications that vendors, developers, and implementation teams can work from. We help you define what you need before you spend, avoiding the costly misalignment between business expectation and technical delivery that derails most IT projects.

Scoping Specifications Procurement

09
IT Trainings for Staff

Practical IT and cybersecurity training delivered to your team — covering security awareness, phishing recognition, safe data handling, device security, password hygiene, remote working security, and incident reporting. Training programmes are tailored to your industry, your specific risks, and your staff's technical level — from non-technical employees to IT team upskilling sessions for specific technologies or platforms.

Why UAE Businesses Choose VOWTECH for IT Consulting

Senior engineers. Practical outputs. UAE context. We deliver consulting that drives real change — not reports that get filed and forgotten.

Senior-Led Engagements

Every VOWTECH consulting engagement is led by a senior engineer with direct, hands-on experience across infrastructure, security, and cloud environments. We don't assign junior consultants to do discovery and then have a senior review it — your engagement is handled by experienced practitioners from day one through to final delivery.

Actionable Outputs — Not Documents

Our deliverables are designed to be used, not archived. Reports are written in plain language with clear priorities, specific recommendations, effort estimates, and suggested owners — giving your team a practical action plan that can be briefed to management and worked through systematically, rather than a lengthy compliance document that requires interpretation.

Deep UAE Context

Our consultants are based in Abu Dhabi and Dubai with direct knowledge of the UAE regulatory landscape, local technology infrastructure, and sector-specific requirements across healthcare, finance, education, and government — ensuring our guidance reflects how UAE businesses actually operate and the specific obligations they face, not generic global frameworks that don't translate to the local context.

We Implement What We Recommend

Unlike pure advisory firms, VOWTECH can remediate and implement the recommendations we make — through project-based work or an ongoing AMC. This eliminates the gap between advice and execution that frequently undermines consulting value, giving you a single accountable partner from assessment through to measurable improvement in your IT environment.

Strict Confidentiality

All engagement data, system access, findings, and documentation are handled under strict NDA and data handling protocols throughout every consulting engagement. Your infrastructure details, security posture, and business information are treated with the same care we expect from our own suppliers — never shared, retained beyond the engagement, or used for any purpose outside the agreed scope.

Ongoing Relationship — Not One-Off Projects

Many of our consulting clients engage VOWTECH on a recurring basis — annual audits, quarterly security reviews, ongoing compliance monitoring — building a relationship where our team develops deep knowledge of your environment and can track improvement over time. This continuity delivers far more value than one-off assessments that lose context between engagements.
