// CAUSES OF DATA LOSS

Why UAE Businesses Lose Critical Data

Data loss can strike any organisation at any time — and without a professional recovery partner on call, even a few hours of downtime can translate into significant operational and financial damage.

01
Hard Drive & SSD Failure

Mechanical hard drives fail due to head crashes, spindle motor failure, and platter degradation — often with little warning. SSDs suffer from flash controller failure and NAND wear. Both scenarios require specialist hardware-level intervention to recover data without causing further damage to already compromised storage media.

02
RAID & Server Array Failure

RAID arrays protecting business data can fail through multiple simultaneous drive failures, RAID controller corruption, accidental rebuild on wrong drives, or failed RAID rebuilds that overwrite recoverable data. Without specialist RAID recovery knowledge, standard recovery tools will destroy the very data you are trying to recover.

03
Ransomware & Malware Encryption

Ransomware attacks targeting UAE businesses have surged significantly — encrypting files, databases, and backups simultaneously. When backups are also compromised or absent, professional decryption analysis, shadow copy recovery, and partial plaintext recovery techniques may be the only path to restoring business-critical data without paying a ransom.

04
Accidental Deletion & Formatting

Accidental file deletion, unintentional partition formatting, and erroneous disk wiping are among the most common data loss scenarios. The critical factor is time — every write operation to the affected storage after data loss reduces the recoverable surface area. Immediate engagement with a recovery specialist maximises your recovery outcome.

05
Power Surge & Physical Damage

Electrical surges, flooding, fire, and physical impact can damage storage media at the hardware level — damaging PCB circuits, read/write heads, and NAND chips. Physical damage requires cleanroom-level intervention and specialist component transplantation to recover data from drives that no longer function in any conventional system.

06
Database & Application Corruption

SQL Server, Oracle, Exchange, and ERP database corruption can render entire business systems inaccessible — affecting years of transactional data. Without database-level recovery expertise, conventional restoration from corrupted backups will simply restore the corruption. We perform deep database file repair and selective data extraction from damaged database files.

// HOW WE WORK

Our Data Recovery Process

A structured five-phase recovery methodology — transparent at every step, with a no-recovery no-fee guarantee on all standard recovery cases.

01
Assessment & Diagnosis

We evaluate the failed device — determining the failure type, extent of damage, and viable recovery approach without performing any action that could cause further data loss. You receive a detailed assessment report with a clear success probability, recovery scope, and timeline before any recovery work begins.

02
Recovery Plan & Approval

Based on the diagnosis, we present a detailed recovery plan including recommended technique, required resources, estimated timeline, and cost — with no obligation. Only after your explicit approval do we proceed with any recovery procedure, ensuring full transparency and control throughout the process.

03
Data Extraction

Our engineers execute the recovery procedure — imaging the source media sector-by-sector to a secure recovery system before performing any logical reconstruction or file extraction. Working from the image protects the original media and enables multiple recovery passes without risk of overwriting or damaging recoverable data.

04
Validation & Verification

Every recovered file set is validated for integrity and completeness before delivery. We provide a comprehensive file listing for your review — enabling you to confirm the critical data you needed has been recovered intact before we proceed to secure delivery, giving you full confidence in the outcome before closing the case.

05
Secure Delivery & Handover

Recovered data is delivered to you on encrypted storage media or via a secure transfer link — with all copies on our recovery systems securely wiped post-delivery. We also provide recommendations to prevent recurrence, including backup strategy guidance and storage health monitoring to protect your business going forward.
